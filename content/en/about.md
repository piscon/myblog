Hi, I write as **Deux** (二重奏, "a duet").

> A duet of spirit and thought, a symphony of freedom and independence.

That subtitle is the whole story behind the name: putting rational engineering and personal observation side by side, letting them play off each other.

## What gets written here

- **AI & toolchains**: engineering notes on making models do real work — this blog itself runs on an AI translation pipeline, publishing in three languages at once
- **Site building & typography**: every customization on this Hugo / PaperMod site is recorded as a reproducible convention, see [all posts](/en/posts/)
- **Life**: observations and essays beyond technology
